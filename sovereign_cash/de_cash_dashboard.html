Notes

  • Buffer = net money-market placements: the Finanzagentur stock 'Kassenverstärkungskredite und Geldanlagen' with the sign flipped (negative = net cash-management borrowing). The Bundesbank pays 0% on federal deposits from Oct-2023, so the central-bank account is liquidity-neutral.
  • Cash requirement = core budget (BMF Finanzierungssaldo, deficit +) + net borrowing booked to the special funds by the Finanzagentur. Fund spending is not observed monthly: cash a fund has borrowed but not yet spent sits in unexplained financing.
  • Core, rest of 2026: spending closes to its Soll €524.5bn × 0.97, revenue to its Soll €426.4bn × 1.02 (each the mean of outturn ÷ plan over 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2019). From 2027 the draft core is scaled by the balance haircut 0.85 over 2022, 2023, 2025; that band is 0.41–0.91. Left out of the balance haircut: 2024 (unexplained financing −€37.2bn) — above €20.0bn, the core outturn misses cash that left the buffer.
  • Special funds through Aug-2026: Infrastruktur 81% / Bundeswehr 72% of plan pace YTD; BW profile=plan_residual_flat (scale_channel=True), implied YE €25.5bn; infra=flat_ytd_pace. Hist month shares only when the Wirtschaftsplan sits inside recent FA scale; a defence / fund ramp (plan ≫ those years) sets YE = plan and flattens the residual — do not freeze last year's Dec spike onto a new level. Wirtschaftsplan is annual only — no published monthly Auszahlungsprofil. Other funds forward 0: their −€5.5bn this year is unscheduled repayment.
  • Plan scenario = core Soll/draft without haircut + plan funds at the plan pace. Issuance is the same in both scenarios, so the difference lands on placements (Dec-2026: model €10.4bn, plan −€23.6bn).
  • Destatis extra-budget deficit €44.1bn YTD to Sep-2026; its monthly change correlates 0.22 with fund borrowing (Aug-2023 → Aug-2026), so it is a cross-check, not an input.
  • Year-end money-market budget financing, repaid in January, counts as net bills: €6.0bn Dec-2024, €13.0bn Dec-2025. Forward 0.
  • The identity closes by construction: other financing = Δ(Schuldschein + other loans) + an unexplained remainder (sd €4.6bn/month excluding 2020–2021). The Finanzagentur's own debt identity closes to €0.000bn.
  • Placement backtest (gap rule identity, gap 0: PRE has 17 h=12 windows (< 36), too few to choose; gap 0 by default): the path beats flat placements at PRE h=1, 3; OOS h=1; loses at PRE h=6, 12; OOS h=3, 6, 12 (OOS h=12: MAE 56.6 vs 28.0). Read the placement path as where cash lands, not as a level forecast beyond a month.
  • Debt moved between a fund and the core budget without a market transaction is not borrowing: WSF −€72.7bn (Jan-2024).
  • Coupons: auction tape to 11-Nov-2026, then the published issuance calendar to 08-Dec-2026, then a pattern sized on the borrowing plan; gross × 0.944 (market share of tape volume). Redemptions = tape ladder + tranches issued before the tape (Bund 6.5% 04-Jul-2027 €11.2bn) × 0.940. No regular buyback programme.
  • Bubills: tape to 09-Nov-2026, then the published calendar (€47.0bn gross to Dec-2026) × 0.971; stock flat after.
  • Eurosystem-held redemptions are modelled: the ECB publishes country stock and maturity, not holdings by line, so lines are allocated with caps of 33% (PSPP) + 50% (PEPP) per line, no reinvestment. Months where the modelled share of redemptions exceeds 60% — Feb-2027 83%, Aug-2027 78%, Nov-2027 76%, Jan-2028 82%, Feb-2028 76%, Jul-2028 78%, Aug-2028 75%, Feb-2029 74%, Aug-2029 73%, Jan-2030 74%, Feb-2030 72%, Aug-2030 63% — sit near the caps and likely overstate runoff.
  • Macro scenario card: primary spending on HICP, receipts on NGDP (AMECO/WEO), interest carved from the ISIN book with +100bp from debt_repricing. Level SARIMAX does not clear PRE. Live core path = bottom-up macro (Soll open-year × hist/YTD blend; later smly×NGDP/wages + coupon×FA-stock interest).

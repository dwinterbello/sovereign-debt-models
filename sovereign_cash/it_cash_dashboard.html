  • Fabbisogno model chosen on PRE (origins 2015–2021) mean MAE: last-year repeat; OOS (origins 2022→) reported only. Scored on the first provisional MEF print, COVID months Mar-2020–Feb-2021 screened out.
  • RRF grants lower the fabbisogno; loans are financing. Grants are estimated from Banca d'Italia EU loans at the plan ratio (37% grants). The base months carry €9.3bn of past grants, stripped before forecasting; next 12m add €10.5bn of grants to the fabbisogno and €17.9bn of loans to other financing.
  • Cash path = seasonal / annual-level (last-year repeat). Forward drivers: AMECO NGDP (DFP Tav. II.1.2.2 where published) and WEO CPI. The DFP total pins the year in progress (2026: €129.5bn = 5.6% of GDP, vintage 2026-04-22) for the year in progress. Later years use our component model, the same path as the Shock scenarios page (cash-account lines on the macro paths, interest from the debt book, RRF grants only as scheduled). Scenario card = model-implied Δ from ±1pp GDP/CPI; live path unchanged.
  • BOT: BOT stock held at its current level plus its average calendar-month change (trailing five years, demeaned); raised only to keep liquidity at the floor. Liquidity above the band (P90 of the last 36 month-ends) is a financing surplus: peak €45.6bn in Jul-27; the Treasury would close it with smaller auctions, buybacks or fewer BOT.
  • Other-financing baseline: zero (PRE mean MAE zero 8.85, trailing 12m mean 10.52, last-year repeat 11.47, level-adjusted seasonal, 3y 13.98bn), next 12m €0.0bn.
  • Gross bonds next 12m include €0.0bn announced syndications / retail and €102.6bn assumed (last year's syndications and retail issues repeated 52 weeks on by the issuance forward).
  • Eurosystem-held redemptions are estimates (holdings by ISIN are not published; snapshot 2026-10-01); next 12m €201.2bn.
  • Identity: history closes by construction (other financing is the residual). Against Banca d'Italia's instrument table (2012-12→2026-08), the part not explained by its non-security financing and perimeter has mean −€0.36bn and sd €6.6bn a month (last 24m sd €7.1bn), vs other financing sd €8.5bn.

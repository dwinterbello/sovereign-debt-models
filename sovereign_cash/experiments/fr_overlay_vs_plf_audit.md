## Verdict

The bare-overlay vs live-BUM comparison is **unfair on OOS, fair on PRE**. Soft-pin α=1 is not the stack — the PLF **category** annual pin is. Even after equalising soft-pin and after switching to BUM whenever PLF categories are known, live BUM α=1 still wins post-2020 OOS. Nothing ships.

## Is “overlay loses to PLF” apples-to-apples?

**PRE — yes, fair on pins.** `authority.json` has no category rows before 2023 and no `state_deficit` solde before 2025. Soft-pin α=1 is inert (BUM α=0 = α=1 = 7.44). The PRE gap (overlay 8.77 vs BUM 7.44) is a different annual constructor: BUM holds last complete SMB/PLF year (+ remboursements_locaux CPI grow) and spreads with 5y shares; overlay scales open seasonals with HICP/equity. Pin/hold does not stack PRE.

**OOS — no, stacked.** From Oct-2022 BUM pins PLF category annuals when known (80% of OOS h=12 origins have some pinned months; mean 6.6 of 12). Soft pin to the solde only starts Oct-2024 (25% of OOS origins; mean 2.1 months) and is thin: BUM α=0 already at 8.64 vs seas 14.50; α=1 only to 8.45. The research overlay had neither pin. Giving it the same soft-pin α=1 only moves OOS 11.55 → 11.05 — still ~2.6 behind BUM. The information edge is the category PLF table, not the residual solde soft pin.

**STATUS PRE win vs seas was sample-sensitive.** On step=3 (the augment script’s grid) the candidate beats seas (~6.9 vs ~7.4). On this dense BUM-matched walk it loses PRE to seas (8.77 vs 8.13). Quote PRE vs seas only on the sample you ship against.

## Spec definitions

- **seas_mult_3** — production seasonal on the SMB deficit.
- **BUM α=1 / α=0** — live `bottom_up_macro` (PLF categories when known, else hold + remboursements_locaux grow; 5y shares; interest smly). α = soft pin to `state_deficit`.
- **overlay alone** — candidate seasonal × macro scales; no PLF.
- **(a)** BUM year totals, months reshaped to overlay within-year shares, then soft-pin α=1.
- **(b)** BUM α=1 + α·(overlay_softpinned − BUM). α=1 = (c).
- **(c)** overlay + soft-pin α=1 to the same PLF solde — fairness check on the residual pin.
- **(d)** per calendar year: BUM months if PLF categories known at origin, else overlay; then soft-pin α=1.

Notes

  • Sign convention: net borrowing requirement and all financing lines + = borrowing (deficit); the budget balance is the same number with the opposite sign.
  • Financing is Riksgälden's net borrowing at nominal amount by instrument; FX and inflation revaluation are removed by the source. Redemptions = gross − net, so they also absorb switches and buybacks.
  • Nominal SGBs: settled and announced auctions, then a 7-day auction calendar (breaks 08 Jul–05 Aug; 17 Dec–14 Jan, public holidays skipped) with one size per year that lands on Riksgälden's plan (2026: 5 × SEK 5.14bn, 2027: 44 × SEK 5.46bn, 2028: 44 × SEK 5.46bn, 2029: 43 × SEK 5.58bn, 2030: 43 × SEK 5.58bn).
  • T-bills: an auction every 14 days on the observed weekday (same breaks rule). Each rolls what matured since the last one plus an even step toward Riksgälden's planned year-end stock; new issues mature on the third Wednesday with the tenor mix of the last 24 months. Liquidity management absorbs the rest.
  • Linkers: the plan spread over the months that had linker auctions in the past year; one FX bond a year in June (month of the latest FX issue). Redemptions from the outstanding list.
  • Stock checks against Riksgälden year-end outcomes: end-2024 bills 112.9 vs 113, liquidity management 133.6 vs 134; end-2025 bills 172.7 vs 173, liquidity management 137.0 vs 137.
  • Timing items excluded from the YTD deviation (SEK +39.8bn balance terms, release 2026-11-06): EU Recovery and Resilience Facility payment SEK 15bn, planned 2026-07, now expected in 2026 (release 2026-08-07).
  • Bottom-up CH1089 baseline: NO-SHIP — PRE anchored mean MAE 10.567 vs production 10.045 (PRE anchored mean MAE bottom-up 10.567 does not beat production 10.045 by ≥5%). Unanchored PRE 10.104 vs 11.277. Alternate bottom-up cash-requirement column does not replace the live path when NO-SHIP.

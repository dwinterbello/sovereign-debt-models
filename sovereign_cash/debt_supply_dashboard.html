How the forecasts are built

Cash-need recipes (UK / DE / FR / IT / ES / NO) feed the financing board; this Debt & supply tab keeps interest on the live bond book and the yield curve. Full category tables live on the Methodology page.

PieceWhat drives it
Interest on this tabEvery country with an auction tape: bond-by-bond coupons, linker uplift, floating-rate resets, the bill ladder rolled on the forward curve, plus the gap between that marketable book and the published interest outturn (loans, APF, definition). New bonds are couponed at the issuance-mix forward yield.
UK cash interestSame ISIN + curve annual figure, spread across months with the usual ONS JW2P seasonal pattern, scaled so the trailing twelve months still match the published total (covers non-marketable pieces the gilt book does not see).
Germany cash interestISIN + curve annual × the Finance Ministry interest month pattern outside the open Soll year (open year still blends the ministry plan with outturn).
France cash interestAFT published debt-service charge when the budget has already printed it; otherwise the ISIN + curve annual. Months follow same-month-last-year.
Italy cash interestISIN + curve annual × Bank of Italy interest month shares.
UK revenue / spend (cash)OBR year levels for each tax and spend block, ONS month shares, then the OBR ready reckoner on live earnings, retail, CPI, output, equities and rates.
France revenue / spend (cash)Budget (PLF) category lines when published; otherwise hold last complete year (local refunds may grow with inflation). Soft-pinned to the official deficit.
Germany / Italy revenue (cash)Germany: open-year Soll blend; later years same-month-last-year with GDP on receipts and wages on social spending. Italy: same-month-last-year with GDP on tax and inflation on primary spending.
SpainCash rest-of-year: IGAE AC tax/social × NGDP and primary spend × HICP (es.bottom_up_macro). Interest on this page is Bonos / Letras ISIN + Tesoro curve (GOV_CURVE). Multi-year fiscal path from EC / Eurostat.
JapanCash rest-of-FY: MoF GA same-month-last-year (category×macro NO-SHIP; sample from FY2024 only). Interest on this page is JGB / T-bill ISIN + MoF curve. Fiscal path Cabinet Office then WEO. Screened 2020–25 interest MAE ~¥177bn vs ~¥417bn last year.

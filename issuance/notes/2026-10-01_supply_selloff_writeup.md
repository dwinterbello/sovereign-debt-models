# Sovereign supply: key themes, tradeables, and the global selloff (1 Oct 2026)

Source: `issuance/issuance_dv01_dashboard.html` and its caches (tape to 23–30 Sep 2026; forward to
Sep 2027). DV01 is million USD per bp. "Private" = gross coupon supply minus official purchases.
Auction yields are bucket averages of nominal auctions, so they carry maturity-mix noise; confirm
spreads on fixed benchmarks before trading.

Forward official DV01 is zero in the UK (APF auctions paused, 17 Sep 2026 notice) and the US (Fed
rolls all principal, 16 Sep 2026 directive). Euro-area QT is passive runoff.

**Germany — euro-area duration glut.** Record private supply, official share still falling, and the
largest imbalance in the set. Bunds cheapen vs swaps (short ASW 2y–10y); front end and belly
underperform the long end. Catalyst: Finanzagentur 2027 plan (December); the forward assumes 2027
Jan–Sep €307.8bn (+10% on 2026) and 84% of German forward DV01 is low-grade until then.

**UK — supply scarcity.** Private DV01 −13%, APF out of the market, imbalance −2.4 (long −3.5).
Long (15y+) supply rebounds +70% but from a trough (£26bn face trailing) and stays below average
(z −0.9 at Mar-27); no ultra (30y+) supply on the forward. 10y ASW 47.7bp (55th pct since 2023),
10s30s 76.5bp (71st pct). Trades: UK–DE 10y spread tightener (both legs agree); gilt ASW tighter
in the medium sector (supply −21%); 10s30s / 30s50s flatteners. Syndication windows (Nov, Jan,
Mar, Jul, Sep) are tactical entry points. Catalysts: DMO minutes 16 Nov, FY27-28 remit Mar-27.

**France — record programme, short-end concentration.** €340bn net 2027 (€385bn gross), synds
€10bn Jan / €8bn Feb / €10bn Apr. Short supply +17%, medium −8%; issuance WAM 10.0 → 9.5y
(z −2.4). Trades: OAT 2–5y cheapening vs Bund and swaps; OAT 3–7y vs Bund wider; relative
flattening of OAT 2s10s / 5s30s.

**Italy — no supply signal.** Flat supply; Q4 pinned by MEF guidance (€64bn); 2027 syndications +
retail €94.5bn. Mild BTP 7–15y cheapening bias only; 87% of forward low-grade until MEF guidelines.

**US — neutral, upside risk on coupons.** Sizes held; long-end DV01 dip is a yield-level effect
(5.3% vs 4.7%). TBAC flags possible FY27 coupon increases: 5s30s steepener optionality into the
4 Nov refunding; unwind if sizes are held.

Ranking of supply pressure: DE > FR short end > IT > US > UK. Cleanest expression: long gilts vs
short Bunds, duration-neutral.

## 2. Relevance in a global rates selloff

**The move.** Nominal auction yields, last month vs three months earlier:

| | UK | DE | FR | IT | US |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <3y | +58 | +48 | – | +90 | +60 |
| 3–7y | +53 | +44 | +56 | +83 | +44 |
| 7–15y | +45 | +44 | +54 | +68 | +45 |
| 15y+ | – | +34 | +38 | – | +39 |

Bear flattening everywhere (DE long−front 75bp vs 89bp 3m ago and 119bp 12m ago; US 57 vs 78 vs
102). A synchronised, front-end-led move is policy/inflation repricing; supply works through term
premium at the long end and does not explain it. Supply is also not driving it now: Q4 gross DV01
vs Q4-25 is UK 0.73×, IT 0.73×, FR 0.82×, DE 0.95×, US 0.97× (partly mechanical: higher yields
shrink DV01 per unit face).

**What the supply data adds:** where incremental duration must clear at a price, and where the
weak points sit.

1. **Italy — clearest stress.** 0–3y BTP +90bp vs Schatz +48bp (≈40bp spread widening); 7–15y
   ≈25bp wider. Supply is light, so this is fiscal/credit repricing. Structural weakness: official
   share 40% → 21% → 18%, so the marginal buyer is price-sensitive (retail, foreign, real money).
   Test: Jan–Feb 2027 at full size (1.0× last year) with the syndication/retail programme.
2. **Germany — safe-haven cushion eroding.** Most private supply since 2015, imbalance +3.6, falling
   official share: Bunds outperform less in a selloff, ASW cheapens. Auction cover fits: short 1.80
   vs 1.96 and belly 1.51 vs 1.61 (3m vs prior 9m) — weaker exactly where supply z is largest and
   the selloff is largest — while long cover improved (2.19 vs 1.99): long-duration demand at higher
   yields is intact.
3. **France — the 2027 programme is the risk, not current auctions.** Covers improved (7–15y 2.74
   vs 2.50); OAT–Bund 7–15y only ≈11bp wider over 3m. Pressure point is Jan–Feb 2027: DV01 1.12×
   last year, record programme start, €10bn + €8bn syndications, short supply z +3.3 by Mar-27.
4. **US — 4 Nov refunding is the event risk.** Covers flat (~2.5); no QT drag. A coupon increase
   into a selloff with 15y+ at 5.36% stacks term premium on top of policy repricing. Only dated
   supply shock inside this quarter.
5. **UK — least supply-exposed.** Q4 0.73×, APF paused, covers up (medium 3.36 vs 3.18), 3m tails
   narrower (0.23–0.27bp vs 0.46–0.58bp), UK–DE 7–15y stable near 200bp. Watch: the last two
   auctions tailed 0.4bp (2032) and 0.5bp (2036), tail z +0.8 / +1.0, with the 2036 clearing
   ≈23bp above August. One more wide tail turns the cushion into a weak point.

**Timing.** Now–December: supply is a relief valve; the move runs on central banks and data.
4 Nov US refunding. December: Finanzagentur / MEF / AFT plans (above-replay plans confirm the
European supply story). Jan–Feb 2027: gross supply resets to full size everywhere with syndications
(UK new 2047, FR, IT programme, DE annual plan) — the window where a selloff most likely becomes a
supply / term-premium problem, with IT and FR the weakest buyer bases.

## 3. Blind spots

- Japan: no JGB supply or BoJ taper in the dashboard, a primary channel for global long-end moves.
- Positioning / flows: auction cover and tail are the only demand read.
- DV01 falls mechanically with yields; near-term "lower supply" partly reflects the selloff.
  Face amounts are what treasuries fix.

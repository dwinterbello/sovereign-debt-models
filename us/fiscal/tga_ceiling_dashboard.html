TGA stockpile and buybacks

Yellen cash policy was about a week of outflows, ~$550–600bn. Bessent Q3 2026 refunding (TBAC) assumes $950bn end-Sep and $850bn end-Dec. 12-month ΔTGA is up, so the stockpile was built, not drained by buybacks. The May 2024 program had two books: cash management (seasonal, spend TGA / cut bills around tax dates) and liquidity support (weekly OFTR; amounts treated as a borrowing need — issuance-financed, not a TGA draw). Using the ~$1tn TGA to fund larger long-end OFTR ops is a 2026 overlay, not the original design. Rebuilding the TGA after a draw requires more issuance later.
